# Isathes Verb Declension — **Current Tense & Mood Inventory (Draft)**

This table shows **all productive verb forms we currently have**, independent of verb class restrictions (those are noted afterward).

---

## 1. Core Tense & Mood Table

### 1.1 Finite Verb Forms (All Classes, Unless Restricted)

|Category|Name|Form|Function|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Unmarked**|Non-past|`Verb`|Present or future state/action|
|**Past**|Retrospective|`Verb-a`|State/action that occurred|
|**Imperative**|Command|`Verb-e`|Command directed at an agent|
|**Invocative (Low)**|Agentless causation|`Verb-i`|Ordinary binding / declarative causation|
|**Invocative (High)**|Ritual agentless causation|`Verb-aen`|Ritual, institutional, or cosmological causation|

---

### 1.2 Example (Class I: _vaeth_ “to bind”)

|Form|Example|Gloss|
|---|---|---|
|Non-past|**vaeth**|binds / will bind|
|Past|**vaetha**|bound|
|Imperative|**vaethe**|bind! (to an agent)|
|Invocative (Low)|**vaethi**|let the binding come into being|
|Invocative (High)|**vaethaen**|let the binding be established (ritual / witnessed)|

---

## 2. Functional Distinctions (Very Important)

### 2.1 Non-past (Unmarked)

- Covers **present and future**
    
- Descriptive or predictive
    
- No causation asserted
    

> _vesal_ → “endures / will endure”

---

### 2.2 Past (`-a`)

- Retrospective
    
- Descriptive only
    
- **Does not undo obligations or states**
    

> _vesala_ → “endured”

---

### 2.3 Imperative (`-e`)

- Commands an **agent**
    
- Requires a being capable of acting
    
- Cannot target reality, existence, belief, or will
    

> _vaethe_ → “bind!” (someone must act)

---

### 2.4 Invocative (`-i`, `-aen`)

- **Asserts causation without an agent**
    
- Does not command
    
- Does not describe
    
- Claims responsibility for the utterance itself
    

|Form|Register|Use|
|---|---|---|
|`-i`|Low|Legal, social, everyday binding|
|`-aen`|High|Ritual, institutional, cosmological|

## 4. What This Table Explicitly Does _Not_ Include (Yet)

Just to be very clear, these are **out of scope for this table**:

- Aspect (ongoing, completed, habitual)
    
- Conditional / hypothetical
    
- Evidentiality
    
- Modal futures
    
- Negation (particle-based)
    
- Cessation constructions (structural, not inflectional)
    

Those will layer _on top_ of this system later.
