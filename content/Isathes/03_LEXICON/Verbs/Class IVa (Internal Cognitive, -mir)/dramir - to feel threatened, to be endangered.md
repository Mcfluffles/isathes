**Class:** **IVa — internal state (-mir)**

**Meaning:**

> To experience oneself as endangered by an opposing condition.

**Key properties**

- internal perception / state
- non-transferable
- cannot be invoked, commanded, or cessate-d
- reflects **subjective or institutional awareness**, not external fact

**Examples**

`ei dramir`

> I feel endangered.

This cleanly separates **external threat** from **internal experience of threat**.
