**Form:** **`dresal`**  
**Class:** **III — Existential / stative (-sal)**

**Meaning:**

> To exist in opposition such that another state’s endurance is endangered.

This is **not causative** and **not volitional** — it is the _existence of danger_.

**Examples:**

`Enemy dresal`

> An enemy threatens / is a threat.

**Nominalization (ritual / scholarly):**

- `dresalir` — threat (as an extant condition)
