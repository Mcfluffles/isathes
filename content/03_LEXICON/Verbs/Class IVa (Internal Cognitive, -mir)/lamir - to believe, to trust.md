**Form:** **`lamir`**  
**Class:** **IVa — Internal cognitive state (-mir)**

**Meaning:**

> To hold confidence or belief in the reliability or integrity of another.

**Properties:**

- internal
- non-transferable
- cannot be invoked, commanded, or cessate-d directly

**Examples:**

`ei lamir`

> I trust / I believe.
