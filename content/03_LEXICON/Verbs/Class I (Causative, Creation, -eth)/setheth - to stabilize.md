- **Class:** #I_causative_creation-eth 
- **Phonology:** /sɛθɛθ/
#### Definition (for notes)

>**setheth** (Class I, -eth): _to cause a state or structure to endure; to reinforce or restore stability._ 

This verb denotes active stabilization and contrasts with **kareth**, which denotes the provision of sustaining support.

#### Examples

`Bond ve setheth`

> (Someone) stabilizes the bond.

#### Nominalization

- **Process:** `sethethir` — the act of stabilizing
- **Result:** (future-defined result nominal) — a stabilized state
